NordPulse Portable Blender — Product Copy

The NordPulse portable blender is a compact personal mixer for smoothies, protein shakes, and soft fruit purees. A 16 oz Tritan cup, a six-blade stainless steel assembly, and a rechargeable base fit into a bag for the gym, the office, or a weekend trip.

Fill the cup to the marked line, lock the lid, and press the button once to start. The motor runs in short pulses; press again to stop. For a smoother texture, add liquid first, then fruit, then ice. The blade assembly and the cup are easy to rinse under the tap; the lid and the seal can be removed for a deeper wash.

The base charges through a USB-C port and shows a small LED ring while charging. A full charge covers several single-serving blends, depending on the ingredients and the run time. Store the unit with the cup open and the blade dry.

In the box: the motor base, the 16 oz cup, the blade lid, a USB-C cable, and a short guide. The guide covers assembly, first use, daily cleaning, and basic troubleshooting. Keep the base away from water, and do not run the motor with an empty cup.

Care notes: hand wash the cup and the lid, dry the blade before storage, and check the seal after each wash. For support, contact the service desk listed on the box. The product is covered by the store return policy; read the label before first use.
